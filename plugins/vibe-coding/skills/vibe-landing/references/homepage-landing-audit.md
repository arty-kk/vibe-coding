# Homepage & Landing Audit

## Goal

Identify evidenced problems that prevent the selected public page from delivering its intended visual, informational and conversion experience. Inspect the complete rendered page and reachable states before proposing changes. Separate defects, missing required evidence and optional creative opportunities.

## Establish the expected page

Read the request, actual offer, accepted design/brand, requirement ledger and relevant source. Identify the audience, primary/secondary paths, route(s), content/asset owners and available renderer. For a broad quality request, establish the page's intended reading/action sequence; do not use a personal style preference as a universal requirement. A source-only inspection can establish a false claim or absent destination but cannot prove a visual hierarchy from CSS values alone.

## Inspect the experience

1. Capture a complete-page view and relevant section/control detail at representative narrow/wide and short-height viewports. Use actual copy, content and loaded fonts/assets. Walk from entry through orientation, proof, evaluation and action, plus the relevant returning-user or secondary-audience path.
2. Check whether the hero communicates the offer and exposes the intended actions without clipping or an unnecessary empty screen. Evaluate header/footer identity, useful destinations and the relationship between their height, content and viewport. Find repeated sections that add no useful meaning and required questions that remain unanswered.
3. Evaluate composition and hierarchy by the task: scale, contrast, placement, proximity, image/background activity and typographic character. Inspect the actual rendered font face, weight and fallback. Weight 800 on a small link and 740 on a large heading is not itself a finding; demonstrate a competing or obscured intended focal role.
4. Inspect internal geometry and finish: shared axes, padding/gap rhythm, text baselines, icon alignment, media bounds, nested corners, borders and consistent action placement. Preserve intended asymmetry; identify accidental misalignment at its owning component or token. Check light/dark bands and the real states of gradients, transparency and shadows.
5. Follow every important visible action to useful content or a real state/effect. Existing IDs, empty portfolio messages and fake success toasts do not establish a working path. Inspect navigation/back/close behavior, form/calculator/pricing states, validation, pending, error, recovery and the relevant persisted/delivered effect using an authorized test environment.
6. Inspect motion and enhancements: meaningful pacing, first-render content, keyboard/touch equivalents, reduced-motion/static paths, control of ongoing movement and procedural/3D lifecycle/performance. A screenshot does not establish timing or cleanup.
7. Check factual/professional copy, locale/count forms, real proof/media, brand/favicon assets, responsive behavior and accessible task completion. Inspect initial/public HTML, metadata/crawler controls, supported schema and actual loading evidence where in scope. Distinguish local checks from public indexing, field performance or recipient delivery.

Use the focused visual, navigation/state, interaction/motion and search/performance checks when they are the missing evidence for a finding. Do not run every subsystem audit for a bounded page problem.

## Findings and handoff

For each qualifying finding, give the route/viewport/state, expected versus observed result, source owner and rendered/interaction evidence, concrete visitor impact, one repair direction and an observable acceptance case. Merge symptoms caused by the same owner. Identify unverifiable aesthetic or functional claims as missing evidence or proposals rather than upgrading them to proven defects.

Audit-only work ends with findings and limits. For an already-authorized correction, hand the selected finding, expected behavior, actual content/assets, original comparison boundary and failed case to **Improve Homepage & Landing Page**. Do not replace an accepted brand or rebuild the stack merely because a new visual direction is possible.
