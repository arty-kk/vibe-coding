# Improve Product & Landing Copy

## Goal

Improve the selected product wording or call to action while preserving factual claims, localization and destination behavior.

## Domain rules

- Change the authoritative content source and all directly affected renderers/catalog entries.
- Prefer concrete outcomes and action labels; avoid unsupported superlatives, urgency, or implementation jargon.
- Keep variables, pluralization, length, markup, and screen-reader context safe for localization.
- Synchronize CTA destination, tracking semantics, and product states with the new wording.

Use the actual offering's vocabulary and intended locale/register. Edit whole sentences where agreement, declension, quantity or variable order requires it. Keep a compact glossary for repeated domain terms and actions. Prefer a short action label and separate nearby explanation; preserve the exact commitment when shortening a button. State messages must distinguish pending, accepted, rejected and uncertain delivery rather than reusing a generic success sentence.

Remove filler labels and production-process commentary from visible strings. Keep source/rationale notes outside product content. A meaningful qualification, approved legal/service condition or domain term remains part of the interface when the visitor needs it. For a new full-page text deck, use [Website Copy & Microcopy](web-page-copy.md); a selected wording fix does not require rewriting the whole page.

Reconcile an invitation with the destination's actual usefulness. Replacing a production placeholder with “not published yet” while retaining links that promise a portfolio leaves that promise unresolved. Within the authorized scope, suppress an optional invitation until its content exists or direct it to a genuinely useful available destination. Otherwise record the remaining functional/content gate explicitly. Do not invent projects, contact details or a live submission path to close it, and do not describe a page with an unavailable required action as ready for release.

## Validation

- Run content/catalog/link/build/render checks.
- Inspect changed copy in every reachable state and representative narrow/wide layout.
- Read changed localized strings in context and exercise relevant count/variable forms. Verify label, helper text, result and action together at their real widths; preserve meaning rather than forcing arbitrary truncation.
- Verify claims against current repository behavior/config and list external legal/product approvals not obtained.

Trace the selected claim or action to its maintained product/content source, update the directly affected languages and artifacts, then render the same user state. Check both what the text promises and what its destination actually does. Prefer deriving shared versions/capabilities from their authoritative manifest/config over manually repeating values. Recheck failures at that source, then finish Patch Checking.
