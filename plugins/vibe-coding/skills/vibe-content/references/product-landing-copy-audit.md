# Product & Landing Copy Audit

## Goal and scope

Audit selected product or landing copy against actual behavior, audience intent, evidence, and conversion path.

## Domain invariants

- Claims, capabilities, prices, plans, limits, permissions, timing, and outcomes match authoritative behavior.
- Labels and calls to action describe the action and destination without dark patterns, ambiguity, or premature success.
- Copy remains concise, accessible, translatable, consistent with product vocabulary, and appropriate to user state.
- Analytics and experiments do not become hidden sources of copy truth or misrepresent causal impact.

## Audit method

1. Trace each changed claim or CTA to product behavior, plan/config, destination, and success/failure state. Follow the destination far enough to establish that it delivers the promised content or action. An existing fragment ID leading only to an unpublished-content placeholder does not satisfy an invitation to view the work.
2. Identify conflicting terminology across UI, docs, emails, pricing, metadata, and support surfaces.
3. Check audience, intent, evidence, legal/privacy sensitivity, localization constraints, and accessibility.
4. Separate messaging opportunities from factual contract defects.

Inspect the actual strings in their complete sentence and rendered component. Check professional domain terminology, locale/register, grammatical agreement, Russian case/count forms when relevant, and consistent names for the same action. Verify dynamic quantities and variables rather than reading only the default value.

Check the hierarchy of heading, benefit, price/result, explanation and action in context. A sentence-length button, a caption that merely names the block, or an instruction to a builder appearing as product copy is a concrete candidate for correction when it harms the task. Record the exact visible state and effect; a difference in font-weight numbers alone does not establish a copy or visual defect. A field placeholder does not replace its label. Preserve necessary limitations and genuine product terminology while identifying filler or leaked process commentary.

## Handoff

For each selected claim or CTA, retain its visible wording, product/version/state source, actual destination and expected user outcome. Pass the concrete mismatch into Improve Product & Landing Copy. A preferred tone is an editorial choice; a wrong version, unsupported capability or misleading success label is a factual defect with an observable repair criterion.
